After 7 long years, I finally made it back to Japan. This was our whirlwind tour of the southern "half" of Japan - 16 nights spread out between Tokyo, Kamakura, Hakone, Kyoto, Hiroshima, and Fukuoka, with a smattering of other cities visited in between.

We visited our friends Sasha and Kathryn, living in Iwade (Wakayama) and Kobe, respectively. I have never ridden so many trains in my life. We explored all around Tokyo, revisited my old stomping grounds in Funabashi, rode two of world's only nine suspended railway systems (the Shonan Monorail and the Chiba Toshi Monorail), played endless arcade games, and lots, lots more. We visited castles in Odawara and Osaka, rode the ropeways through Hakone, dyed our own fabrics in Kyoto... it was awesome.
