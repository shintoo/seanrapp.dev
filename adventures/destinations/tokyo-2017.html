Did a semester abroad as a Junior in college, studying Japanese mostly, at Meikai University in Urayasu. I lived about a 12 minute walk from Higashi Funabashi station on JR's Chuo-Soubu line. Very much enjoyed going to Taito arcades, the shrine up the street from my dorm, and exploring Tokyo. Absolute best spot was the observation deck at Odaiba. Also enjoyed a ~weeklong trip to Kyoto during Christmas.
