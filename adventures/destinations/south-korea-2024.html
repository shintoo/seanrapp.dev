Flying into Busan from Fukuoka (because the ferry was out of commission...), after two and a half weeks in Japan, Korea felt like an entirely different country. (Wonder why!) Not being able to speak the language was one thing, but much about Korea was very different. We started our journey by staying at Gwangalli Beach in Busan, which is now one of my favorite places in the world. The street that follows the beach is packed with endless things to do, from all kinds of restaurants and extremely cozy 3-story cafes, to all kinds of arcades. We thoroughly enjoyed sitting on the beach with a snack, watching the light show on the Gwangalli Bridge at night reflect off of the waters in the bay.

Then, we took the KTX intercity high speed train to Seoul, for the last few days of our epic adventure. We visited the palace grounds, went to the top of Lotte World Tower, visited our friend Kaitlin, and explored one of the coolest cities in the world.
