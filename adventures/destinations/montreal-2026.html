A quick weekend trip to get out of the country and enjoy a beautiful city. We stayed in the old port for two nights. Between the two of us we've barely spent two weeks in Europe altogether, so it was such a treat to get some of that feeling just an hour and a half flight away. We ate muffins in the morning under the trees at the Place d'Armes, crawled through crowds up and down Rue St-Paul Ouest as Cabo Verde scored a goal against Argentina, and picnicked by the clock tower on the St Lawrence river.

It was warm, but not too - especially considering it was hitting the 100s back home. We climbed Mont Royal and got engaged at the top, with a beautiful view of the city. It happened to be the world's largest jazz festival that weekend, which we got a peek at when we ran through downtown to a pharmacy closing in a few minutes to buy sunscreen.
